How scene generation works

  1. Write your story in the Story field.
  2. Add visual style and creative rules in Overall Direction.
  3. AI plans scenes to fit your target duration.
  4. Click Analyse Story, then map each story character name to a saved Studio character or let AI invent it.
  5. AI invents characters only when no saved character is assigned or detected.
  6. AI generates a cinematic still image for each scene; you can replace it by generating again or uploading your own image.
  7. Edit, save and export your storyboard.
