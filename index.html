How scene generation works

  1. Write your story in the Story field.
  2. Add visual style and creative rules in Overall Direction.
  3. AI plans scenes to fit your target duration.
  4. Click Analyse Story and assign every detected character to an existing saved Studio character.
  5. Only your saved character library is used. No automatic character creation.
  6. AI generates a cinematic still image for each scene; you can replace it by generating again or uploading your own image.
  7. Edit, save and export your storyboard.
Production workflowAI assists with character analysis, scene planning and scene artwork. Character identities are supplied by your saved 360° reference images. Video motion generation uses the configured video provider.
